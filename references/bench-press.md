# Bench press analysis rules

## Evidence model

Do not use “straight up and down” as the ideal. In a side view, an efficient press commonly shows a diagonal/J-shaped screen path: descent toward the lower chest/feet, then press up and back toward the shoulder/rack.

Track the visible bar hub and label `start`, `descent`, `touch`, `press`, and `lockout`. Calculate:

- top-to-touch horizontal displacement as a percentage of plate diameter;
- lockout return error relative to the start x;
- direction and smoothness around the sticking region.

These describe the observed path; they are not universal target numbers.

## Joint and body observations

Track wrist, elbow, and shoulder consistently. At touch and early press, inspect wrist-over-elbow stacking in the camera plane, forearm orientation, touch point consistency, elbow path, scapular stability, upper-back contact, leg-drive timing, butt contact, bounce/sink, and lockout.

Do not prescribe extreme elbow tuck or a single touch point without considering grip width, limb lengths, arch, ruleset, and symptoms. A 30–45° view can help show joints but introduces parallax; state this limitation.
